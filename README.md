# Sacrificing Souls  

A **2D platform runner game** created during the **Daydream Srinagar Game Jam** under the theme: **"Sacrifice Must Be Made"**.  

Built with the **Godot Game Engine**, *Sacrificing Souls* explores the idea of trading strength for progress—where survival comes at the cost of giving something up.  

---

## 🎮 Gameplay  
- **Level 1:** Classic platforming—run, jump, and navigate hazards to reach the goal.  
- **Level 2:** The twist—your character **loses one of the abilities**, forcing you to adapt and master the challenge with fewer options.  

Every step forward demands a **sacrifice**, testing your reflexes and adaptability.  

---

## ✨ Features  
- 2 handcrafted levels of progressive challenge.  
- Unique ability-loss mechanic tied to the theme of sacrifice.  
- Minimalist yet engaging design created within the time limits of a game jam.  
- Built entirely in **Godot**.  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack  
- **Engine:** [Godot](https://godotengine.org/)  
- **Language:** GDScript
